What we store

  • On your device: your name, start date, daily feelings (check-ins), optional daily notes, future-self letters and their writing/opening dates, favorite images, language and widget settings.
  • In your account (after you sign in with Google): your Google account identifier and email address, the 30-day trial dates, and a private copy of your name, start date, feelings, daily notes, future-self letters (including unopened letters and their dates) and favorites so you can recover them on a new phone.
  • Trial history after account deletion: we retain a keyed hash of your email address and the trial dates, so the free trial cannot be repeated.

Your daily feelings, notes and letters may contain sensitive personal information. Writing is optional. We use it only to provide the app's display, storage and backup features. We do not send your writing to AI services or include it in analytics or debugging logs.

Future-self letters are hidden in the app until their selected date, but this is a display restriction, not encryption or a secure delivery service. Manual backup files contain the full text of notes and letters, including unopened letters. Keep these files private.

What we do not do

We do not show ads or sell or share your data for marketing. RevenueCat generates purchase statistics as described below.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase hosts the account database and authentication.
  • Google provides Google sign-in. Google Play processes purchases; we never receive your card details.
  • RevenueCat processes your user ID and purchase history to verify and restore access and generate purchase statistics.

Retention and your rights

Account data is kept until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or by following the steps on the delete account page. Deleting your account removes your account, your trial record and your cloud copy, including daily notes and letters. Writing stored on your device stays until you remove it or uninstall the app; exported backup files must be deleted separately. Signing in again can back up writing still on your device. RevenueCat purchase records are not automatically deleted when you delete your account; contact us to request their deletion. You may also ask us for access to or correction of your data by email.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Each account can only read and write its own cloud copy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
